Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Applies to the Manotu apps for Android and iOS and the Manotu service.

In short: your personal places and history stay on your phone. We never store them on our servers. We only receive what you choose to share publicly or send us (feedback, crash reports), plus anonymous speed points while you navigate, which we turn into road traffic within minutes and never keep.
Ringkasnya: tempat dan sejarah peribadi anda kekal dalam telefon anda. Kami tidak menyimpannya di pelayan kami.

1. Data that stays on your device only

Home and Work, favourites and their labels, recent destinations, search history, places you add "just for me" and their photos, planned drives and settings are stored only in the app on your device. Manotu's servers do not receive or store them.

2. Backups (your storage, your choice)

You can back up that on-device data to your own Google Drive (a hidden app folder only Manotu can access, scope drive.appdata), your own iCloud private database, or a file you export. Backups go straight from your device to that storage. They never pass through Manotu's servers, and we cannot read them. You can protect a backup with a passphrase that encrypts it on your device (Argon2id + XChaCha20-Poly1305). We never see the passphrase, and if you lose it, the backup cannot be recovered.

Manotu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Manotu accesses only its own backup files in your Drive's app folder, only to save and restore your backups, and transfers that data to no one.

3. We never know who you are

Your public contributions are credited to a random pseudonym that only your phone and your own backup can sign for. We never ask for your email, phone number, name or a Google/Apple login to use Manotu. Your contributor tier (Baharu, Perak, Emas) describes your public work, not you. When you vote on whether a community-added place exists, the vote is linked to your pseudonym only until the place is decided; after that it is unlinked from you. The same goes for votes on road and place complaints. Road reports and the "still there?" votes on them are unlinked from you about an hour after the report expires (the votes are deleted), so we keep no history of where you reported things. Verifier identities are never shown to anyone.
Kami tidak pernah tahu siapa anda: sumbangan anda dikreditkan kepada nama samaran rawak. Kami tidak pernah meminta e-mel, nombor telefon atau nama anda untuk menggunakan Manotu.

5. If you leave your email or WhatsApp

When you send feedback or a crash report, you may choose to leave an email address or WhatsApp number so we can reply. It is optional and asked for per report. It is used only to reply about that report, is never linked to your pseudonym or profile (a report that carries a contact is stored without any link to your account), and is stored encrypted. It is deleted automatically 30 days after the report is closed, and in any case after 90 days. You can withdraw it at any time from that report in the app.
Jika anda memilih untuk meninggalkan e-mel/WhatsApp pada laporan, ia hanya digunakan untuk membalas laporan itu dan kemudian dipadam.

6. What we never do

No advertising, no tracking or analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data, no building of location history about you. Crash reports are sent only if you agree, never contain your location, and go only to us, not to any third-party crash service.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete any on-device data in the app, delete your backups from your own Drive/iCloud, and delete your Manotu account in the app (Menu → Account → Delete my Manotu account). That removes every server record linked to you; your public reports, places, reviews and photos stay on the map but are anonymised. Details, and how to ask us by email if you no longer have the app: manotu.app/delete. You may also contact us to access or correct data we hold, consistent with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

8. Open source

9. Children

Manotu is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. Contact: privacy@manotu.app.
