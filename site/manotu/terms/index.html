1. Using Manotu

Manotu provides maps, routes, traffic information and community reports for Malaysia. Use it as an aid only. Always obey traffic laws, road signs and the police, and keep your eyes on the road. Do not handle the phone while driving; set your destination before you move, or let a passenger do it.

Road safety. Never drive when tired or sleepy, or under the influence of alcohol, drugs or medicine that makes you drowsy. If a direction looks wrong or unsafe, follow the road, not the app. You are responsible for how you drive, and to the extent permitted by law the developer of Manotu is not responsible for accidents, injury, damage or fines.

Keselamatan jalan raya. Patuhi undang-undang jalan raya, papan tanda dan polis. Jangan memandu ketika letih atau mengantuk, atau di bawah pengaruh alkohol, dadah atau ubat yang mengantukkan. Jangan pegang telefon; tetapkan destinasi sebelum bergerak. Jika arahan nampak salah atau bahaya, ikut keadaan jalan. Anda bertanggungjawab atas cara anda memandu dan, setakat yang dibenarkan undang-undang, pembangun Manotu tidak bertanggungjawab atas kemalangan, kecederaan, kerosakan atau saman.

3. Community rules for your contributions

When you publish a road report, a complaint, a public place, a review, a photo or a display name, you confirm it is accurate to the best of your knowledge, is your own, and does not infringe anyone's rights. You keep ownership, and you grant Manotu a worldwide, royalty-free licence to store, display, adapt (e.g. resize photos) and distribute it as part of the service. You can delete your places, reviews and photos at any time; road reports and complaints expire on their own and are anonymised.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Not allowed: hateful, harassing, threatening, sexual, violent or otherwise offensive content; abuse of other users; false or misleading reports; spam or advertising; reviews of your own business or of competitors; personal information about others (e.g. faces or number plates as the focus of a photo); illegal content; and attempts to manipulate traffic data or votes.

Restricted and sensitive places. Do not add, photograph, review or describe military, security or strategic sites (protected areas and places under the Protected Areas and Protected Places Act 1959), shelters, safe houses or anyone's home, and do not post security details of any place (guards, CCTV, fences, entrances, patrol times). The app refuses contributions in and next to these areas, and holds others for review. Authorities can ask us to remove content at privacy@manotu.app; we act within 24 hours.

Reporting. Use Report on any review, photo or place in the app, or email privacy@manotu.app. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically. We review reports and act within 24 hours. You can also hide a contributor's content on your own phone.

Removal and banning. We remove content that breaks these rules, and we ban accounts that post it or abuse other users: a banned account's public content is hidden and it can no longer contribute. New accounts made to get around a ban are banned too.

Jangan tambah, ambil gambar, ulas atau terangkan kawasan tentera, keselamatan atau strategik, rumah perlindungan, rumah selamat atau rumah sesiapa, dan jangan siarkan butiran keselamatan mana-mana tempat (pengawal, CCTV, pagar, pintu masuk, waktu rondaan).
