Delete your Manotu account

Padam akaun Manotu anda · Applies to the Manotu apps for Android and iOS

Manotu has no email or phone login. Your account is a random pseudonym that only your phone (and your recovery code) can sign for. Your personal places and history were never on our servers: they live on your phone.
Manotu tiada log masuk e-mel atau telefon. Akaun anda ialah nama samaran rawak. Tempat dan sejarah peribadi anda tidak pernah berada di pelayan kami.

1. In the app (fastest)

Open Manotu → Menu → Account → Delete my Manotu account, and confirm. It takes effect immediately.
Dalam aplikasi: buka Manotu → Menu → Akaun → Padam akaun Manotu saya, kemudian sahkan. Ia berkuat kuasa serta-merta.

2. What is deleted

  • Your account: pseudonym, display name, mood, public signing keys, device check keys, tier, points and counters.
  • Your inbox, your "helpful" marks and the reports you made about other people's content.
  • Your votes that are still linked to you (on places, complaints and road reports still being decided).
  • Screenshots attached to your feedback, and any old server copy of saved places or recents from early app versions.

Yang dipadam: akaun anda (nama samaran, nama paparan, kunci tandatangan, tahap, mata), peti masuk, tanda "membantu", laporan anda tentang kandungan orang lain, undian yang masih dikaitkan dengan anda, dan tangkapan skrin maklum balas.

3. What stays, anonymised

Public contributions stay on the map for everyone, without your name or any link to you: road reports, road and place complaints, places you added, edit suggestions, reviews and photos. Feedback text and crash reports also stay without any link to you (feedback is deleted after 24 months, crash reports after 90 days). If you want a review, photo or place removed rather than anonymised, delete it in the app before deleting your account, or email us.
Yang kekal tanpa nama: laporan jalan, aduan, tempat yang anda tambah, cadangan suntingan, ulasan dan foto. Maklum balas dipadam selepas 24 bulan dan laporan ranap selepas 90 hari.

4. Email or WhatsApp you left on a report

A report that carries a contact is never linked to your account. Its contact is deleted automatically 30 days after the report is closed, and in any case after 90 days. You can withdraw it sooner from the report in the app.
E-mel atau WhatsApp pada laporan tidak dikaitkan dengan akaun anda dan dipadam secara automatik (30 hari selepas laporan ditutup, paling lama 90 hari).

5. Data on your phone and in your backups

Home and Work, favourites, recents, search history, private places and planned drives are only on your phone. Uninstall the app (or clear its data) to remove them. Backups you made to your own Google Drive or iCloud, or exported files, are yours: delete them there.
Data dalam telefon: nyahpasang aplikasi untuk memadamnya. Sandaran dalam Google Drive atau iCloud anda perlu dipadam di sana.

6. No longer have the app?

Reinstall Manotu, restore your account with your 24-word recovery code, then delete it as above. If you can't, email privacy@manotu.app with your display name and the contributions you want removed. We reply within 30 days. Because accounts have no email or phone, we can't prove an account is yours without the recovery code, but we will remove any content you point us to.
Tiada aplikasi lagi? Pasang semula Manotu dan pulihkan akaun dengan kod pemulihan 24 perkataan, atau e-mel privacy@manotu.app dengan nama paparan anda. Kami membalas dalam 30 hari.
