## Positioning

**Ma’no tu? Ke mana pun, bersama Manotu.**
A Malaysian community navigation app in early access. Friendly, useful, Malay-first; short English support.
Use the approved Manotu wau icon and outlined wordmark, cobalt #0047AB, coral #FF6B4A, ink #0B1B3A and pale #E3ECFA.
Keep social artwork simple and readable at phone size. Use actual app recordings for feature demonstrations; never fabricate app screens.

## Launch and learning

Prepare profile, cover, About, support link and first three posts together. Reply to useful comments within one working day when possible. Do not promise live support coverage. Ask for feedback inside the app after the person has stopped safely.
Track only aggregate platform metrics: reach, saves, shares, profile visits and link clicks. Compare the four themes after two weeks; repeat the themes that produce useful feedback and saves. No tracking pixels, advertising SDKs or precise location analytics are introduced by this kit.

## Copy corrections for the source Marketing canvas

Apply throughout that Marketing page (not historical app package names or legacy compatibility URLs):

- Public brand “Gimano” → “Manotu”.
- bukitbunga.com/gimano → manotu.app; github.com/erymuzuan/gimano → github.com/erymuzuan/manotu.
- Facebook link-preview hostname BUKITBUNGA.COM → MANOTU.APP.
- Asset/source reference site/gimano/index.html → site/manotu/index.html.
- Replace “every line / entire app is open source” with **Kod tulisan Manotu berlesen MIT. Komponen platform Google dan Apple turut digunakan.**
- Remove unverified “tiada teknologi Israel” supply-chain claims; point to documented dependencies instead.
- Replace “raw traffic points deleted in 30 seconds” with **Isyarat trafik tanpa nama diproses sementara; perjalanan peribadi anda tidak disimpan.** The promise does not establish a universal 30-second deletion deadline.
- Qualify cloud backups by build: **Pilihan sandaran bergantung pada versi aplikasi; eksport fail tersedia.** Google Drive is absent from the Play build; do not advertise it as universal.
- Replace broad “Manotu never stores any personal data” with the specific favourites/search-history promise and link to current privacy terms. Public contributions and the approved encrypted event relay are separate cases.
- Do not claim one tap sends a flood report unless verified against the released flow. Say **Lapor keadaan jalan selepas berhenti dengan selamat.**
- Keep early-access language. Do not promise complete traffic coverage, guaranteed routes, road safety, or unsupported app-store availability.
